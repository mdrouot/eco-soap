About EcoBrand

At EcoBrand, we believe that small choices can make a big difference. We combine natural plant ingredients with sustainable ideas to create handmade soaps. By giving used coffee grounds a second life and reducing unnecessary waste, we aim to make everyday care more environmentally friendly.
